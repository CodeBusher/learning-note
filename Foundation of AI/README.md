## How to study

1. Start with the NEU lecture concepts, examples and derivations as the **primary course source**.
2. Read the linked Berkeley CS188 textbook sections for supplementary intuition, alternative formulations and further examples.
3. Independently reproduce the equations/proofs and solve a variation.
4. Update [progress.md](progress.md) after demonstrating mastery.
