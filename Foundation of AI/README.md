# Foundation of AI

Personal study notes for **CS 5100 Foundations of Artificial Intelligence**, taught by **Prof. Nadim Saad** at **Northeastern University, Silicon Valley Campus**.

The chapters introduce notation before using it, explain the reasoning behind formulas, and walk through concrete solutions. The course lecture materials remain the primary source; linked Berkeley CS188 readings provide supplementary explanations. Added teaching examples illustrate the ideas without claiming to reproduce a particular lecture.

## Study chapters

| Chapter | What you will learn |

| [Markov Decision Processes](03-mdps.md) | Define policies and returns; build Bellman equations; calculate utilities |

## How to use these notes

1. Read the definitions and symbol meanings before the equations.
2. Follow each derivation one line at a time and identify the rule that justifies it.
3. Work through the numerical examples, then change an input and recompute the result.
4. Use the worked review to connect topics and the progress checklist to record mastery.

Each chapter ends with one **Sources and references** section. Supplementary extensions are identified in the text. Worked solutions are available immediately; covering them to practice independently is optional.
